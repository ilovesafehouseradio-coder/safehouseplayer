Safehouse Radio app: Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Safehouse Radio is a non-profit internet radio station. This page says, in plain words, what the Safehouse Radio app does with information.

Who we are

Safehouse Radio is responsible for the information described on this page. You can contact us at ilove@safehouseradio.co.uk.

What stays on your phone

The DJs and shows you follow, your reminder choices, your settings, and saved copies of the schedule, DJ list and pictures so the app opens quickly. None of this is sent to us.

What the app asks the internet for

The radio stream and what is on air, our public show calendar, and DJ names, pictures and pages from our website. As with any request on the internet, those servers can see your device's network address.

What the app does not do

The app has no sign-in of its own, no adverts and no tracking or analytics. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Nothing about you is sold or shared.

Permissions the app asks for

Notifications: to show what is playing and to send your show reminders. Internet and network state: to play the radio. Background playback and keeping the phone awake while playing: so the radio keeps playing with the screen off. Starting with the phone: so reminders are planned again after a restart.

The chat

The Chat tab shows our website's chat inside the app. The chat is run for us by the provider that hosts our website, who handles chat information on our behalf and only to run the chat. If you use it, the chat collects a display name; an email address and password if you create an account (guests can join with just a display name, and email and picture are optional); a picture, if you choose to add one; and the messages you post, which others in the room can see. It also keeps a sign-in on your phone so you stay signed in.

Your choices and your information

Sign out and clear chat data: in More, under Chat account, this removes your saved chat sign-in from your phone straight away. Delete your chat account: in More, under Chat account, "Delete my chat account" opens an email to us. We will arrange for your account and the details linked to it to be deleted within 30 days, and confirm by email. You can also email ilove@safehouseradio.co.uk at any time. Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Casting

If you cast the radio to a speaker or TV, your phone uses Google's Cast service. We do not receive any information from it.

Reminders

Reminders are planned on your phone from the public schedule. Nothing is sent to a server to make them.

Changes

If we change this page, the date above changes too.

How to delete your chat account

In the app: open More, then Chat account, then "Delete my chat account". It opens an email to us. Or email ilove@safehouseradio.co.uk from the address you used for the chat, with your display name. We will arrange for your account and the details linked to it to be deleted within 30 days, and confirm by email.
